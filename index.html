Community & How GTA 6 Cheats Work

GTA 6 locks cheats behind story completion and individual unlock challenges rather than letting you enter every code from a brand-new save.

Save Warning: Activating cheats can disable saving, trophies, or achievements for that session—always save your main progress first.

How to Enter Codes on Console

  • Unlock First: Complete the campaign and meet each cheat's unlock challenge so it is available in your playthrough.
  • Button Input: Open your in-game phone and enter the directional arrows and face/shoulder buttons in order.
  • PS5 & Xbox: Switch between PS5 / PS5 Pro and Xbox Series X|S above to view matching face button symbols.

Unlock Requirements & Challenges

Some cheats unlock after finishing the main story, while others require shooting range medals, workouts, store robberies, or discovering vehicles.
