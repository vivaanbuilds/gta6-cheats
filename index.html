How GTA 6 Cheats Work

GTA 6 locks cheats behind story completion and individual unlock challenges rather than letting you enter them from the start of a new playthrough.

Important Save Warning: Activating cheats disables saving, trophies, and achievements for that session—save your main file first.

How to Enter Codes on Console

  • Unlock First: Finish the main story and complete each cheat's unlock requirement to open the Cheats tab under Progress.
  • D-Pad Input: Press D-pad Up to open your phone, hold L1 + R1 on PS5 / PS5 Pro or LB + RB on Xbox Series X|S, and enter the D-pad arrows.
  • Same Sequences: PS5, PS5 Pro, and Xbox Series X|S use the same D-pad arrow sequences.

Unlock Requirements & Challenges

Some cheats unlock after finishing the campaign, while others require shooting range medals, workouts, store robberies, or discovering specific vehicles.
